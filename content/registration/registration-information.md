## Registration fees

There are four types of registration available: Early bird, Student, Low income
country, and Regular bird, and two registration options: Member and Non-member.
The prices for each type of registration are listed in the tables below.

{{< callout icon="sparkles" >}}
**Early bird registration is available until the 8th of May 2026, 23:59 AEST.**
{{< /callout >}}

### For Members

The Member registration option is open to everyone, including people who are not
already members of the ISE (International Society of Ecoacoustics). The
registration fee **includes** the cost of ISE membership, so if you select this
option, you will automatically become a member of the ISE as part of your registration
process.

For more information about ISE membership, please visit the [ISE website](https://sites.google.com/site/ecoacousticssociety/contact-us)
