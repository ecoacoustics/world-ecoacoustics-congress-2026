**Call for Workshops is now closed. Thank you to everyone who submitted a proposal.**

We strongly encourage submissions from delegates in developing nations and with
diverse backgrounds, as we aim to foster a diverse and inclusive program. See
guidelines and suggested themes below, but please feel free to suggest themes
not listed below.

## Submission guidelines

Each workshop should have a lead which will be responsible for organising
communication with participants. Proposals should be 250 words maximum outlining
the relevance of the workshop for the audience. You will be required to include
the expected number of participants and material needed. Workshops will be held
on Monday (17/08/2026) and they can be half or full day workshops. Please keep
in mind that there are a limited number of rooms for workshops.

<u>**Submissions close on the 1st of March 2026.**</u>

Themes below are suggestions based on community requests, feel free to suggest
other workshops that might be relevant.

## Suggested themes

- Recogniser building  
- Standardisation of PAM metadata
- Annotation protocols  
- Working with large datasets  
- Global databases of annotated soundscapes  
- Dealing with uncertainty: how to incorporate uncertainty around machine detections in reporting  
- Making ecoacoustics truly accessible: how can more privileged pockets of the community support others?
